Petition: Changing an Act about Wildfire Management

To: THE HONOURABLE THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF THE PROVINCE OF BRITISH COLUMBIA, IN LEGISLATURE ASSEMBLED:

WHEREAS:

  • British Columbia is experiencing increasingly severe, frequent, and devastating wildfire seasons due to shifting climate patterns, which threaten human lives, public health, wildlife, critical infrastructure, and our natural ecosystems;
  • The current Wildfire Act and Wildfire Regulation focus heavily on reactive fire suppression and post-incident liability rather than proactive, landscape-scale ecological restoration and preventative fuel management;
  • Modern wildfire resilience requires structural transitions toward long-term, full-time employment in forest management, enhanced deployment of localized prescribed and cultural burning, and stricter ecosystem-based land management mandates; and
  • Relying primarily on reactive seasonal operations no longer adequately protects British Columbians or our forests from catastrophic loss.

    • Sign this petition to demand that British Columbia gives its wild bison a secure future. Let our leaders know that we value ecological integrity, biodiversity, and the preservation of our iconic Canadian wildlife.

Petitioners Request to:

  • Mandate Year-Round Forest Management:Transition structural frameworks to support permanent, full-time employment for wildfire personnel dedicated to year-round forest restoration, selective fuel thinning, and proactive risk reduction.
  • Expand Cultural and Prescribed Burning:Establish clear legal pathways and long-term, distinctions-based government-to-government frameworks with First Nations to expand the regular use of cultural and prescribed fire stewardship.
  • Prioritize Ecosystem Resilience:Re-evaluate and amend land-use policies within the Act to legally prioritize ecosystem health, biodiversity, and community climate resilience above short-term economic or corporate timber values.
  • Enhance Community Protection Zones: Legislate mandatory, standardized fire-buffering zones and proactive fuel management around all high-risk wildland-urban interfaces across the province.
