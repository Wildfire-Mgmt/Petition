To the Honourable the Legislative Assembly of the Province of British Columbia, in Legislature Assembled:

The petition of the undersigned, residents of British Columbia, states that:

Since 1917 there have been 24,751 documented wildfires that have burned a total area of 22,086,836 ha across British Columbia. From 2020 to the end of 2025, 2,387 fires burned 6,659,404 ha and as of August 24, 2026, an additional 255 fires have burned 422,258 ha. Many rural and remote communities, as well as urban centres, have been on evacuation alert and subsequently under evacuation order. Many homes have been lost, values impacted, and lives displaced. Many local, rural and remote residents stay to defend, however, this often results in significant conflict especially during evacuation orders. We seek for the Good Samaritan Act to be revised to equally include wildland fire response in a similar manner as emergency medical services.

On November 8, 2023, the BC Emergency and Disaster Management Act changed to allow increased enforceability during evacuation orders which could inadvertently criminalize or penalize local grassroots volunteers and property owners defending their own communities, placed heavier documentation, training, and operational requirements (such as Hazard, Risk, and Vulnerability Analysis programs) on local authorities and volunteer groups without guaranteed long-term funding.

In light of the extensive amount of wildfire and interactions with rural and remote communities in British Columbia it is our vision that we as British Columbians need to be supported to stay and defend with the appropriate training, resources, and structure. We, the people of British Columbia, seek to take increased responsibility for ourselves, our neighbours, and our communities as we stand shoulder to shoulder facing increased wildland fire and a need for local resources led by provincial agencies where they are present and where not present, to be empowered to render emergency response within the bounds of wise practices bolstered by reformed legislation.

Therefore, your petitioners respectfully request that the Honourable House urge changes to the Good Samaritan Act [RSBC 1996] Chapter 172 and the BC Emergency and Disaster Management Act.

Dated first day of September, 2026.
